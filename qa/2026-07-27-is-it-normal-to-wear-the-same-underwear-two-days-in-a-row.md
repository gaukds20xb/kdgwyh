# Is it Normal to Wear the Same Underwear Two Days in a Row?

Once again, Reddit is asking the important questions: Just days after a confused wife reignited the conversation around whether or not it is normal for men to pee sitting down, another Reddit relationship post has sparked debate around men’s underwear habits.

“Just learned that my boyfriend goes for days without changing his underwear and I’m horrified,” the post reads. “Is this a normal dude thing?”

[Two Days in](https://peplux.cc/category/kj8wnyu)

Many guys replied that they change their undies every time they shower, which is usually every day (excluding camping trips or the occasional hangover day), with one guy commenting: “If I don’t change my boxers it’s usually depression.”

While a daily shower and change seems to be the norm for these guys, at least one in five men aren’t doing that. In May 2015, sociology research by the University at Maryland found that 28 percent of men believed that wearing the same underwear two days in a row was completely fine. And who among us hasn’t had a lazy Sunday where we lounge around in our pajamas or whatever we were wearing the day before?

But as one Reddit commenter put it, there is an all-important caveat: “Going a few days without a shower is fine. Taking a shower and then putting your dirty underwear back on is deviant.”

More than that, it’s also bad for your health. Everybody’s underwear contains traces of bacteria and fecal matter, which can cause a pretty bad smell. Not to mention the build-up of sweat and dead skin cells, which in combination with chafing and overgrowth of yeast can irritate the skin, leading to itching, unpleasant rashes, and even fungal conditions like a staph infection or tinea cruris, commonly known as jock itch. This applies doubly if you’re the sweaty, active type.

[the Same Underwear](https://pepvora.com/frs4yv8npe/)

And if you sleep in your smalls, you’re even more susceptible to itchy skin conditions. Responding to a study on the health risks of unwashed sleepwear, professor Sally Bloomfield from the London School of Hygiene said: “You shed skin cells at a vast rate all the time. They are full of microorganisms. We all have skin and gut organisms that are usually not harmful on our skin and in our gut. But if they get into the wrong place, they can cause problems.”

In brief, fresh boxers are best. Don't count on the sniff test to help you, either: Bacteria may be present even without a smell, and you don’t want to transfer the E.coli coming from your rear end to your face, says Kelly Reynolds, Ph.D., director of the Environment, Exposure Science, and Risk Assessment Center at the University of Arizona. But if it's an underwear emergency, you can probably wear the same pair for up to three days, max, advises Philip Tierno, Ph.D., professor of microbiology and pathology at the NYU School of Medicine.

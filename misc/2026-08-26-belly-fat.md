[Belly Fat](https://science.bairibu.com/blog/1273646291.html)

Too many people don't get started on big projects, like losing weight, because the task seems too big and insurmountable.

But even if there's a long way to go to lose your gut, a recent research review gives you all the incentive you need to get started.

After all, having a lot of belly fat, specifically the fat around your organs known as visceral adipose tissue (the fat that is most often found in those "hard beer bellies" of older men) is deadly.

[Belly Fat](https://pepvora.com/category/f3lli)

Australian researchers reviewed 61 studies and found that modest weight loss resulted in a preferential loss of visceral adipose tissue (VAT).

Reference: International Journal of Obesity (2008) 32, 619–628;

What that means to you, is that as soon as you start a fat loss program, the fat you lose is often that deadly belly fat. So just by getting started and losing a few pounds, you can make a dramatic improvement on your overall health.

# The First Trailer for 'Hobbs & Shaw' Is Finally Here

Dwayne "The Rock" Johnson is getting ready to unveil his eagerly anticipated Fast & Furious spinoff movie, Fast & Furious Presents: Hobbs & Show. After the star released a new movie poster Thursday, the first trailer hit the internet on Friday.

In the trailer, we see Luke Hobbs (Dwayne Johnson) and Deckard Shaw (Jason Statham) teaming up to take down villain Brixton (Idris Elba)—while also dealing with their long-simmering beef. There are people running down the sides of skyscrapers, vehicles flying through the air in ways that no vehicles ever should, and everything else you'd expect from a movie in wild Fast & Furious universe.

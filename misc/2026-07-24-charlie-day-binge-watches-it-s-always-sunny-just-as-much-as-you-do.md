# Charlie Day Binge-Watches 'It's Always Sunny' Just As Much As You Do

Charlie Day is one of those successful guys who knows that he's successful, but tends to forget it from time to time. "I was out with my wife the other day and I said, 'I can't ever tell when I'm in a situation where people recognize me or don't recognize me.' And she says, 'Honey, everyone in this room recognizes you.' So what do I know."

Charlie is off sequel duty on Pacific Rim Uprising, out Mar. 23, in which he reprises his role as Dr. Newton Geiszler. ("Not sure what I can say about the plot. I don't want to get sued. I can say there's probably gonna be some awesome action music.") Following that, he's back to write and star in the improbable 13th season of It's Always Sunny in Philadelphia, which still qualifies as a cult hit even though it's one of TV's longest-running comedies.

Day plays Charlie Kelly, and the two share more than just the first name. "I think Charlie and I have some similarities," he says. "We look and sound almost exactly alike. People mistake me for him all the time. We both share a love of music. I don't enjoy cheese as much. I used to, but I think I'm lactose intolerant. What else? We both fell in love with a waitress. And I think we both like beer."

[As You Do](https://pepitverte.com/cu1o9p7wqu/)

If the real Charlie has any life advice, it would be this: Every once in a while, "play in a different sandbox." Giant robot vs. monster movies are a big departure from low-budget comedy, and for Day that's the point. "It changes up your brain a bit," he says, "and reminds you there's other stuff out there."

# Kevin Hart Is Looking Jacked in New 'Borderlands' Workout Photos

Kevin Hart has been busy getting into character for his next project—with the help of some weights. The comic actor just showed off his jacked biceps in a carousel of photos on Instagram taken from his training for the upcoming sci-fi adventure movie Borderlands.

Hart will be playing the soldier Roland in the adaptation of the video game series, a character who is, to put it bluntly, swole as hell. And if his arms are any indication, Hart is more than prepared. "So ready to take on the role of Roland in 'Borderlands,'" he wrote in the caption. "It's go time people!!!!! Hard work brings great results."

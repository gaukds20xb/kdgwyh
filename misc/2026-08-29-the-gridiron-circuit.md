At 6'4" and 300 pounds, Gerald McCoy is one of the most formidable players on the Tampa Bay Buccaneers. As a hard-charging defensive tackle, he's a lethal combination of extreme power, strength, and quickness during games. So how does McCoy stay in gridiron shape during the offseason? His trainer Todd Durkin, C.S.C.S, author of The IMPACT! Body Plan runs the Pro Ball player through grueling, fast-paced circuits that test both his willpower and athletic abilities.

Here's how to do it: Grab a stopwatch and time yourself completing the following exercises back-to-back, without rest.

Clean to PressStand grasping a barbell just beyond your shoulders, using an overhand grip. Bend at your hips and knees, letting the bar hang just above the floor in front of your shins. Explode up, shrug your shoulders, and roll your wrists back to “catch” the bar at your shoulder girdle, palms facing forward, as you dip down with your knees. Press the bar overhead. That's 1 rep. Do 10.

PullupHang at arm’s length from a chinup bar, using an overhand grip that’s slightly wider than your shoulders. Pull your chest up to the bar and squeeze your shoulder blades together. Pause, and slowly lower your body to the starting position. Perform 10 repetitions.
